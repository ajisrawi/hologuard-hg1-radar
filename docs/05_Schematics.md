## How it is built

- **Symbols.** Official KiCad symbols are used wherever they exist: STM32G474RETx, LT3045, MT25QU, NEO-M9N, MDBT50Q (nRF52840), ISM330DHCX, LIS2MDL, connectors, passives, mounting holes. Parts without a KiCad symbol live in the project library `board/kicad/HG1.kicad_sym` (33 symbols, from [`hg1_symbols.py`](../board/schematic/hg1_symbols.py)).
- **Pins taken from the layout.** Every pin that is routed on the PCB (RFIC RF/LO/IF pads, AD9083 VIN/SERDOUT balls, GaN PA RFIN/RFOUT, ADAR1000 RF pads, PLL/VCO/amp pads) carries the layout's pad number. All six RFICs, six ADCs, six PAs and both beamformers were made pad-identical first by mirroring the left-hand parts.
- **Connectivity.** Every connected pin carries a global net label, every unused pin has a no-connect flag, and a PWR_FLAG is added automatically to every power net without a driver. Net names are identical to the PCB.
- **Board-to-board links.** These use the same pin-out and net names on both sides:
  - J2/J3 (JESD204B mezzanines; lanes taken from the routed PCB, PA current-sense lines on J3 spares);
  - J4 (control: SPI, chip-selects, chirp trigger, SYNC, refclk/SYSREF, MCU SPI to the ADAR1000s, TX mute, drain enable, I²C, GNSS RF, Remote-ID RF, −5 V bias, RF reset);
  - J5 FFC (PHY MDI pairs, RS-485, PPS, discretes, enables, sense lines, −5 V, 3.3 V);
  - the Micro-Fit power links.
- **MCU pin map.** The STM32G474 pin map is the one in [`firmware/mcu/target/bsp_stm32g4.c`](../firmware/mcu/target/bsp_stm32g4.c). It was corrected to the real LQFP-64 pin-out: 13 ADC1 inputs, COMP1 on PB1, TIM1_CH1 drain switch on PA8, I²C3 on PC8/PC9, and HSI clock.
- **Scripted "Update PCB from Schematic".** Running [`board/generate_boards.py`](../board/generate_boards.py) after the schematic:
  - links every footprint to its symbol (UUID path) and copies values and every pad net from the KiCad netlist;
  - stages the 65 / 48 / 45 not-yet-placed footprints (passives, INA186 current-sense amps, regulators, drain switch and so on) on a grid beside each board, as KiCad's own update does.

  DRC is still **0 errors**. The ratsnest that remains (495 / 499 / 197 connections) is the real remaining layout work.

## Contents by board

- **ANT:**
  - 54 radiating elements + Remote-ID patch + cal probe as `Device:Antenna` symbols (excluded from board and BOM);
  - 6 × HRX-8 RX RFIC, 6 × AD9083, per-RFIC LT3045 and per-ADC ADP7159;
  - 2 × ADAR1000 driving 6 GaN PAs, with PA_BIAS → gate;
  - per-PA 10 mΩ shunt + INA186 current sense, NTC + ADS7138 pedestal temperatures;
  - P-MOSFET drain switch with gate drive;
  - 2 × ADF4159 + HMC733 + HMC903 chirp chains, HMC7044 JESD clocking, 100 MHz OCXO, TPS62913 1.0 V ADC core.
- **DSP:**
  - Versal XCVE2302 (functional symbol, 300+ pins), 2 × LPDDR4 (A/B), eMMC, QSPI;
  - DP83867 GbE PHY with its XO, PS_REFCLK XO;
  - STM32G474 with SWD, NTCs, rail sense dividers and TPS3700 −5 V window comparator;
  - NEO GNSS, MDBT50Q BLE, nRF7002 Wi-Fi Remote-ID, IMU + magnetometer;
  - TPS546D24A × 2 (VCCINT / VCC_AIE, PMBus), LT8640S 3.3 V, TPS62913 1.8 V / 1.1 V, LDOs for VCCAUX, GT rails, PHY;
  - U.FL OTA-cal port, JTAG.
- **PWR:**
  - M12-A input, SMCJ36CA TVS, LT4363 surge stopper with pass FET and sense resistor;
  - DM filter + hold-up, LT8640S 12 V (DSP) and 5 V (RF);
  - LT8390 4-switch buck-boost 28 V with 4 FETs, LT3462 −5 V;
  - THVD1450 RS-485, GbE magnetics to M12-X;
  - sense dividers, chassis bond, heater switch.

## Status and limits

- **Provisional pin numbers.** The Versal, LPDDR4, eMMC, AD9083, ADAR1000, HMC7044, ADF4159, HMC733, HMC903, DP83867, nRF7002, TPS546D24A, LT8640S, LT8390, LT4363 and ADP7159 symbols sit on proxy footprints (marked `PROVISIONAL` in the symbol description). Pin numbers must be replaced with the vendor package files before fabrication.
- **Simplifications.** Differential pairs (IF, JESD lanes, clocks) are drawn single-ended, and decoupling is only partly populated.
- **Board-to-board mating.** The Samtec LSHM is hermaphroditic and the FFC may mirror. Pin-n-to-pin-n mating is assumed and must be verified on the connector drawings.
- **BLE Remote-ID.** The MDBT50Q has an on-module antenna that will be shielded inside the housing. It needs a U.FL module variant fed from the ANT Remote-ID patch through a diplexer.
